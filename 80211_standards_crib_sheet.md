# 📶 802.11 Wi-Fi Standards – Crib Sheet

A quick reference guide for the IEEE 802.11 wireless standards, covering frequency bands, max data rates, range, and typical use cases.

---

## 📘 802.11 Standards Overview

| Standard   | Release Year | Max Speed      | Frequency Band     | Modulation / Notes                  |
|------------|--------------|----------------|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 802.11     | 1997         | 2 Mbps         | 2.4 GHz             | DSSS or FHSS                         |
| 802.11a    | 1999         | 54 Mbps        | 5 GHz               | OFDM; less interference              |
| 802.11b    | 1999         | 11 Mbps        | 2.4 GHz             | DSSS; longer range                   |
| 802.11g    | 2003         | 54 Mbps        | 2.4 GHz             | OFDM; backward compatible with b     |
| 802.11n    | 2009         | 600 Mbps       | 2.4 & 5 GHz         | MIMO, channel bonding (20/40 MHz)    |
| 802.11ac   | 2013         | ~6.9 Gbps      | 5 GHz               | MU-MIMO, wider channels (80/160 MHz) |
| 802.11ax   | 2019         | ~9.6 Gbps      | 2.4 & 5 GHz (Wi-Fi 6) | OFDMA, BSS coloring, Target Wake Time |
| 802.11ax   | 2020+        | ~9.6 Gbps      | 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6E)    | Extended band; requires new hardware |
| 802.11be   | Upcoming     | >30 Gbps (est.)| 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 7)     | 320 MHz channels, Multi-link Ops     |

---

## 📡 Frequency Band Comparison

| Band     | Pros                              | Cons                          |
|----------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 2.4 GHz  | Longer range, better wall penetration | Crowded, lower throughput     |
| 5 GHz    | Higher speeds, less interference   | Shorter range                 |
| 6 GHz    | Clean spectrum, high performance   | Requires Wi-Fi 6E+ hardware   |

---

## 🛠 Key Terms

- **MIMO**: Multiple Input Multiple Output – improves throughput.
- **OFDMA**: Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiple Access – divides channels efficiently (used in 802.11ax).
- **MU-MIMO**: Multi-User MIMO – serves multiple users simultaneously.
- **BSS Coloring**: Reduces co-channel interference.
- **Beamforming**: Directs Wi-Fi signal more precisely to a device.
