# Dao of Life — Document Retention & Destruction Policy

> Purpose: to ensure that records are retained for appropriate periods, disposed of securely when no longer needed, and preserved when a **litigation hold** is in place. Applies to records in any format (paper, electronic, email, cloud).

---

## 1) Responsibilities
- The **Secretary** is the Records Officer. Each department head is responsible for compliance within their area.
- All personnel must comply with **litigation holds** issued by counsel or the Secretary; routine destruction must be suspended for records covered by a hold.

## 2) Retention Schedule (minimums)
**Permanent**
- Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, amendments, IRS determination letter & Form 1023, Board/committee minutes, major policies, audit reports, real estate deeds, endowment agreements, IP registrations, key contracts, historical program records.

**10 Years**
- Significant contracts and grant agreements after expiration; accident/incident reports with injury; insurance policies after expiration; legal files after close (unless otherwise advised).

**7 Years**
- General ledgers, journals, trial balances; financial statements; accounts payable/receivable; bank statements/reconciliations; canceled checks; payroll records; W‑2/1099 and payroll tax filings; **Form 990‑T** and related tax records; contribution records and acknowledgments; grant files (from final report/close); vendor contracts (after expiration).

**4 Years**
- Routine correspondence; volunteer applications/waivers; routine program files; marketing collateral drafts.

**1–3 Years**
- Internal meeting notes (non‑minutes), drafts and working papers not needed for historical or legal purposes; routine operational emails.

> If another law or contract requires a longer period, the longer period controls.

## 3) Electronic Records & Backups
- Electronic records follow the same schedule. Email that qualifies as a record must be saved to the appropriate repository.
- Backups are for disaster recovery; they are not a records archive. Destruction on backup media follows normal rotation schedules unless a litigation hold applies.

## 4) Destruction
- Paper: cross‑cut shredding or secure shredding service.  
- Electronic: secure deletion methods appropriate to the system; ensure destruction of media before disposal.

## 5) Suspension for Litigation, Audit, or Investigation
- Upon notice of a **litigation hold**, affected records must **not** be altered or destroyed until the hold is released in writing.

## 6) Compliance
- Violations may result in disciplinary action. Questions should be directed to the Secretary.
