# Dao of Life — Donor Acknowledgment Templates

> Use these fill‑in templates to issue **contemporaneous** acknowledgments. For any single donation **$250+**, donors need a written acknowledgment. For **quid‑pro‑quo** gifts > $75, disclose the value of benefits. For **non‑cash** gifts, **do not** state a value (describe the property). Donors should consult their tax advisor.

---

## 1) Cash Contribution (No Goods/Services Provided)
**Subject:** Thank you for your gift to Dao of Life

Dear **[Donor Name]**,

Thank you for your **cash contribution of $[Amount] received on [Date]** to **Dao of Life**, a nonprofit religious and charitable organization. **No goods or services were provided** in exchange for this contribution.

Your gift supports our mission to advance peace, inclusion, worker solidarity, climate‑positive living, and local gardening initiatives that fight hunger.

Sincerely,  
**[Name]**  
**[Title]**  
**Dao of Life**  
EIN: **[XX-XXXXXXX]**

---

## 2) Cash Contribution (Quid‑Pro‑Quo / Event Ticket)
**Subject:** Your contribution and event benefits — Dao of Life

Dear **[Donor Name]**,

Thank you for your **payment of $[Amount] received on [Date]**. In exchange, you received **goods/services valued at $[FMV]** (e.g., event admission, meal). The **tax‑deductible portion** of your contribution is **$[Amount − FMV]**.

We appreciate your support of Dao of Life’s programs for food equity and community education.

Warmly,  
**[Name], [Title]**

---

## 3) Non‑Cash Property Contribution (Describe Only)
**Subject:** Thank you for your in‑kind gift to Dao of Life

Dear **[Donor Name]**,

Thank you for your **non‑cash contribution** received on **[Date]** consisting of **[detailed description of property, e.g., “20 garden tools and 50 seedlings”)**. **No goods or services were provided** in exchange for this contribution.

As required, Dao of Life did **not** provide a valuation of the donated property. If you plan to claim a deduction, please consult your tax advisor regarding **IRS Form 8283** (and any appraisal requirements).

Gratefully,  
**[Name], [Title]**
