[[Dao of Life Hub]]

Seven ready-to-use donor acknowledgment letter templates (cash gift, quid-pro-quo/
event ticket, non-cash property, in-kind professional services, grant/DAF
distribution, recurring-donor year-end summary, restricted/purpose-specific gift) —
used by the `dao-of-life-donor-letter` skill. See "Donor acknowledgment" in
[[Dao of Life Finances]] for the legal reminders baked into them.
