# Using an iPad as a Second Monitor with Windows 11

This guide explains how to use your iPad as a second monitor for a Windows 11 computer using various tools.

---

## ✅ Recommended Options

### 🔹 1. Duet Display (Best Overall Experience)

**Turns your iPad into a second monitor via USB or Wi-Fi.**

#### Setup Steps:
1. **On your iPad**:  
   Download **Duet Display** from the App Store (~$10 one-time).

2. **On your Windows 11 laptop**:  
   Download the Duet Display desktop app:  
   [https://www.duetdisplay.com](https://www.duetdisplay.com)

3. **Connect iPad via USB cable** (recommended for performance).

4. Launch Duet Display on both devices — your iPad will become an extension of your Windows desktop.

> 💡 No noticeable lag over USB. Wi-Fi works too but may introduce latency.

---

### 🔹 2. Splashtop Wired XDisplay (Free Trial or Lower Cost)

A reliable alternative with good performance.

#### Setup Steps:
1. **On your iPad**:  
   Download **Splashtop Wired XDisplay** from the App Store.

2. **On your Windows 11 PC**:  
   Install the desktop agent:  
   [https://www.splashtop.com/wiredxdisplay](https://www.splashtop.com/wiredxdisplay)

3. Connect your iPad using a USB cable and open the app.

4. Your Windows desktop should extend to the iPad automatically.

> 💡 Offers a free trial. Less polished UI than Duet, but works well.

---

### 🔹 3. SpaceDesk (Free, Wireless Option)

Lets you use the iPad as a wireless display, but requires a stable network.

#### Setup Steps:
1. **On your Windows 11 PC**:  
   Download the SpaceDesk server:  
   [https://www.spacedesk.net](https://www.spacedesk.net)

2. **On your iPad**:  
   Install the **SpaceDesk Viewer** app from the App Store.

3. Connect both devices to the **same Wi-Fi network**.

4. Launch SpaceDesk on both and start the connection.

> ⚠️ May have slight lag — best for secondary reference material, not real-time interaction.

---

## ✅ Best Choice:
**Duet Display** is the most seamless and reliable option for most users.
