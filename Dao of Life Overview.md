#daooflife

[[Dao of Life Hub]]

## Mission
Per the Articles of Incorporation (Article Third): organized exclusively for religious,
charitable, and educational purposes under IRC 501(c)(3). Advances peace, inclusion,
worker solidarity, climate-positive living, hunger relief, and sustainable food systems
through worship/assembly, spiritual teaching, education, mutual aid, and community
programs including local gardening.

## Core philosophy
The Dao of Life is a spiritual path and religious organization dedicated to peace, harmony,
worker solidarity, environmental sustainability, and the elimination of hunger and poverty.
Worship exists to affirm these values, strengthen community and mutual support, reconnect
with the Earth and its cycles, and channel collective energy into service and justice.

**Forms of worship:** Community Gatherings (weekly/monthly services centered on creed,
reflection, fellowship) · Seasonal Rituals (planting/harvest ceremonies, seasonal circles,
community meals) · Meditation on Solidarity (guided meditation, compassion and balance
practice) · Acts of Service (gardening, food distribution, education, and climate-positive
community action, treated as worship itself).

**The Dao of Life Creed:**
> We walk in peace, choosing harmony over conflict. We stand in solidarity with workers,
> honoring the hands that feed, build, and clothe us. We live with care for the Earth,
> committed to sustainability and balance. We reject hunger and poverty, and work so all
> may share in dignity and abundance. This is the Dao of Life: a path of compassion,
> justice, and shared humanity.

## Practices & rituals
Regular services follow a five-part structure (opening ritual & creed, teachings/readings,
meditation, an act of service, closing ritual) and the liturgical year turns on four seasonal
rituals tied to the equinoxes/solstices (Planting, Summer Justice, Harvest, Winter Reflection).
Full service structure, the seasonal-ritual table, ministerial training path, and special
services (vigils, celebrations, memorials, dedications) are documented in
[[Dao of Life Rituals and Liturgy]].
