---
name: dao-of-life-board-resolutions
description: >
  Draft formal board resolutions and signed meeting minutes for a Dao of Life board
  meeting — the actual legal adoption paperwork, not the casual gathering notes
  dao-of-life-ops logs. Trigger on requests like "draft minutes for the first board
  meeting," "we adopted the bylaws, record the resolution," or "prep the board meeting
  agenda from the resolutions packet."
---

1. Read the real templates fresh from Drive: `Dao_of_Life_Initial_Board_Resolutions_
   Packet_Template.md` (fileId `1t7NNqUB69jetUWC5luXRfMCXCIMT9khl`) and
   `Dao_of_Life_Organizational_Meeting_Minutes_Template.md` (fileId
   `1DNja4YAjRRN8gmBYiQ9hshD1crQnf3OH`) using the Drive connector's read_file_content —
   don't work from a paraphrase.

2. Check [[Dao of Life Governance]]' "Next steps to formalize governance" checklist to
   see which items this meeting is meant to cover — it may be the full first
   organizational meeting (all items) or a later meeting adopting/amending just one or
   two things.

3. Ask for the real specifics before drafting anything final: meeting date/time/location,
   directors present/absent, quorum confirmation, and — for each resolution being acted
   on — the actual vote result. Never invent names, dates, or vote counts; leave a field
   blank and flagged if the user doesn't have it yet.

4. Draft the minutes note as `Dao of Life Board Minutes YYYY-MM-DD.md`, tagged
   `#daooflife #boardminutes`, following the structure of the real Minutes template,
   with each adopted resolution's actual text pulled from the Resolutions Packet.

5. Link the new note from the "Board minutes" section in [[Dao of Life Meetings]] and
   from [[Dao of Life Governance]].

6. Update [[Dao of Life Governance]] to reflect reality once resolutions are actually
   adopted: change the relevant "drafted template, not yet Board-adopted" language to
   note the adoption date and link to the new minutes note, and check off the
   corresponding item(s) in the "Next steps to formalize governance" list.
