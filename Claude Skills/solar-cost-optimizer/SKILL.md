---
name: solar-cost-optimizer
description: >
  Estimate and reduce the cost of a solar project — equipment/vendor price
  comparisons, incentive and tax-credit research, and payback-period estimates.
  Trigger on "how much will solar cost me," "what incentives/tax credits apply," or
  "help me lower the cost of my solar system." Different from solar-designer, which
  picks equipment for performance/fit, not price.
---

1. Read `Solar Power - System Design.md` and `Solar Power - Site Layout.md` (linked
   from [[Solar Power]]) for the equipment spec and panel count before estimating —
   don't price a system that hasn't been sized.

2. Estimate cost by line item (panels, inverter, battery if applicable, mounting,
   electrical/labor, permitting) using WebSearch for current market pricing — solar
   equipment costs and incentive programs change often enough that training data is
   unreliable here.

3. Research applicable incentives/tax credits and rebates for the user's stated
   location (federal, state/local, utility) via WebSearch — verify against current
   official sources rather than assuming rates that may have expired or changed.

4. Compute a rough payback-period estimate (upfront cost minus incentives, divided by
   estimated annual savings) and flag the key assumptions driving it (electricity
   rate, usage, incentive eligibility).

5. Write or update `Solar Power - Cost Estimate.md`: itemized cost, applicable
   incentives with source/date noted, and payback estimate. Tag `#solar`. Link it
   under a "Costs & Incentives" label in [[Solar Power]].

6. Flag clearly that these are estimates, not quotes, and that incentive eligibility
   should be confirmed with a tax professional or installer before committing.
