---
name: dao-of-life-teaching
description: >
  Draft or update Dao of Life teaching/doctrine content in this vault. Trigger when the
  user asks to write, draft, or expand a teaching, sermon, or doctrinal note for Dao of
  Life, or to update its mission/philosophy in Dao of Life Overview.md.
---

1. Read [[Dao of Life Overview]] for the current mission/philosophy statement and
   [[Dao of Life Teachings]] for the index of existing teachings, so the new content
   is consistent with what's already established and doesn't duplicate it.

2. If the teaching draws on a specific source tradition (Daoist or otherwise) and needs
   research or synthesis beyond what's already in the vault, delegate to the
   `dao-of-life-research` agent rather than guessing at doctrine or inventing quotes.

3. Draft the teaching as its own note: `Dao of Life Teaching - <title>.md`, tagged
   `#daooflife`, linking back to `[[Dao of Life Teachings]]` and `[[Dao of Life Overview]]`.
   Use plain prose sections (e.g. Theme, Teaching, Source/tradition notes, Discussion
   questions) — match the tone of any existing teaching notes if present.

4. Add a link to the new note in [[Dao of Life Teachings]]' index list.

5. If the request is to update the mission/philosophy itself rather than a single teaching,
   edit [[Dao of Life Overview]] directly instead of creating a new note.

6. Follow this vault's conventions: `[[wikilinks]]` between notes (not markdown links),
   no invented member/donor/financial data, and don't restructure existing hand-written
   content beyond what was asked.
