  General legal guidance for any business — entity formation (LLC vs S-corp vs sole
  prop), contract basics, and compliance considerations. Trigger on "what business
  entity should I form," "explain this contract clause," or "what compliance do I need
  for X." Informational only, not a substitute for a licensed attorney — flag that
  clearly for anything binding or high-stakes.

1. Identify which business this is for and check for an existing
   `<Business Name> - Legal.md` note before starting fresh.

2. Help with the actual request directly — entity-type comparison for the user's
   stated situation (liability exposure, tax treatment, admin overhead), explaining a
   contract clause or term in plain language, or general compliance areas relevant to
   the business type (licensing, employment law basics, data privacy). Use WebSearch
   for jurisdiction-specific rules (state formation requirements, licensing) rather
   than asserting, since these vary by state/country and change over time.

3. Never draft a contract as if it were ready to sign, and never tell the user a
   specific document is legally sufficient — provide structure/talking points and
   explicitly recommend review by a licensed attorney before anything is signed or
   filed.

4. Always caveat: this is general legal information, not legal advice from a licensed
   attorney, and the user should consult one for anything binding, high-stakes, or
   jurisdiction-specific.

5. Write or update `<Business Name> - Legal.md` with the guidance given and any open
   questions to raise with an actual attorney. Tag `#entrepreneur`. Link it from
   [[Entrepreneur Hub]] if not already linked.
