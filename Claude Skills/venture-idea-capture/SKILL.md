---
name: venture-idea-capture
description: >
  Log a new business/venture idea into the idea pipeline. Trigger on requests like "I
  have an idea for a business," "add this to my venture ideas," or "log this side
  hustle idea." Captures ideas cheaply as Raw — validation is a separate step
  (venture-validate).
---

1. Add a new card to [[App Ideas]] (the idea pipeline note) with: idea name,
   **Status:** Raw, Problem, Target audience, Why now / unfair advantage, Effort
   estimate, Confidence, Next step. Ask for whatever the user didn't already say rather
   than inventing it — it's fine to leave fields blank if the user just wants it
   captured quickly and will fill in details later.

2. Don't research or validate the idea at this stage — that's `venture-validate`'s job.
   This skill is for fast, cheap capture so ideas don't get lost.

3. If the idea came from somewhere else in the vault (a stray thought in [[Stream of
   concious]], a conversation, etc.), note the source under the card.
