name: entrepreneur-fundraiser
description: >
  Fundraising strategy for any business — investor pitch prep, loan options, grant
  prospecting, or crowdfunding approach. Trigger on "help me raise money for my
  business," "find grants/loans for X," or "prep my investor pitch." Different from
  entrepreneur-business-plan, which drafts the plan itself, not the strategy for
  funding it.
---

1. Identify which business this is for and check for an existing
   `<Business Name> - Fundraising.md` note before starting fresh. Read the business's
   `<Business Name> - Business Plan.md` if it exists, so fundraising strategy is
   grounded in the actual business rather than generic advice.

2. Clarify with the user if not already given: how much they're trying to raise, what
   it's for, and which funding path they're considering (equity investors, debt/loans,
   grants, crowdfunding) — or help them decide if undecided.

3. For the relevant path(s), give concrete next steps: investor pitch structure and
   what investors at this stage typically expect; loan types and typical
   qualification criteria; grant programs the business may qualify for; or
   crowdfunding platform choice and campaign structure. Use WebSearch for current
   grant programs, loan products, or investor landscape specifics rather than
   asserting stale information.

4. If prepping an investor pitch, draft or critique the actual pitch narrative
   (problem, solution, traction, ask) rather than just listing generic slide topics.

5. Write or update `<Business Name> - Fundraising.md` with the strategy, target
   sources/programs identified, and next steps. Tag `#entrepreneur`. Link it from
   [[Entrepreneur Hub]] if not already linked.
