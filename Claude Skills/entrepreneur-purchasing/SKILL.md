---
name: entrepreneur-purchasing
description: >
  Help a business make a specific purchasing/procurement decision — vendor
  comparison, build-vs-buy, or negotiating leverage for one buy. Trigger on "help me
  choose a vendor for X," "should I buy or build this," or "what should I negotiate on
  this contract." Different from entrepreneur-ops-optimizer, which looks at the whole
  workflow, not a single purchase decision.
---

1. Identify which business this is for and check for an existing
   `<Business Name> - Purchasing.md` note before starting fresh.

2. Get the specific need from the user: what's being purchased, budget range, and any
   must-have requirements — don't compare vendors for a vaguely-defined need.

3. Research and compare concrete options (vendors, products, or build-vs-buy) using
   WebSearch for current pricing and offerings rather than relying on potentially
   stale training data — vendor pricing and product lineups change often.

4. Recommend one option with reasoning, not just a neutral list — name the tradeoff
   being accepted (cost vs feature, support quality, lock-in risk). Flag negotiation
   leverage points if relevant (competing quotes, contract term length, volume).

5. Write or update `<Business Name> - Purchasing.md` with the decision, options
   considered, and reasoning. Tag `#entrepreneur`. Link it from [[Entrepreneur Hub]]
   if not already linked.
