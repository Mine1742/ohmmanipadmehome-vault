---
name: greenhouse-price-optimizer
description: >
  Estimate and reduce the cost of a greenhouse project — material/vendor price
  comparisons, cheaper alternatives, and total cost estimates. Trigger on "how much
  will this greenhouse cost," "find a cheaper option for X," or "help me lower the
  cost of my greenhouse build." Different from greenhouse-designer, which picks
  materials for performance/fit, not price.
---

1. Read `Greenhouse Design.md` and `Greenhouse Build Plan.md` (linked from
   [[Greenhouse]]) for the current structure, materials, and build steps before
   estimating — don't price a design that hasn't been settled.

2. Estimate costs by line item (framing, glazing, foundation, doors/vents, irrigation,
   labor if applicable) using WebSearch for current market pricing rather than stale
   training data — greenhouse material costs shift with lumber/polycarbonate market
   conditions.

3. For each major line item, surface at least one cheaper alternative (different
   material, DIY vs prefab kit, used/surplus sourcing) with the tradeoff it costs you
   (durability, insulation, labor time) — don't recommend a cheaper option without
   naming what's given up.

4. Write or update `Greenhouse Cost Estimate.md`: itemized estimate, total, and the
   cheaper-alternative tradeoffs considered. Tag `#greenhouse`. Link it under a "Cost"
   label in [[Greenhouse]] — add that label if it doesn't exist yet.

5. Flag clearly that these are estimates, not quotes — recommend the user get actual
   vendor quotes before committing to a purchase.
