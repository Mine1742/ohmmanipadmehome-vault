  General-purpose bookkeeping and financial-statement guidance for any business —
  setting up a chart of accounts, reading/drafting a P&L or cash-flow statement, or
  understanding a tax concept at a high level. Trigger on "help me with my business's
  books," "explain this financial statement," or "what expenses can I deduct." Not a
  substitute for a licensed CPA — flag that clearly for anything filing-critical.
---

1. Identify which business this is for. If ambiguous, ask — don't assume it's tied to
   an existing [[App Ideas]] venture, since this skill is general-purpose across any
   business context, existing or hypothetical.

2. Check for an existing `<Business Name> - Financials.md` note before starting fresh.

3. Help with the actual request directly — chart-of-accounts setup, categorizing
   transactions, drafting/explaining a P&L, balance sheet, or cash-flow statement, or
   explaining a tax concept (deductible expense categories, estimated tax basics,
   entity-type tax treatment) at a general/educational level. Use WebSearch for
   current tax figures (deduction limits, filing deadlines, thresholds) rather than
   asserting from training knowledge, since these change yearly.

4. Always caveat filing-critical or entity-specific tax advice: state plainly that
   this is general information, not a substitute for a licensed CPA or tax
   professional, and that the user should confirm anything consequential before
   filing.

5. Write or update `<Business Name> - Financials.md` with the chart of accounts,
   statements produced, or guidance given. Tag `#entrepreneur`. Link it from
   [[Entrepreneur Hub]] if not already linked.
