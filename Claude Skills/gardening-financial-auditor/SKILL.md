---
name: gardening-financial-auditor
description: >
  Track and audit actual garden spending (seeds, soil/amendments, tools, water) against
  estimated harvest value, as an ongoing ledger. Trigger on "log this garden expense,"
  "how much am I spending on my garden," or "is my garden worth it financially."
  Different from greenhouse-price-optimizer, which estimates a one-time greenhouse
  build cost rather than auditing ongoing garden spend.
---

1. Check `Gardening - Cost Log.md` (linked from [[Gardening]]) before starting fresh —
   this is a running ledger, not a one-time estimate.

2. If the user is logging an expense, append it: date, item, cost, category (seeds,
   soil/amendments, tools, water/utilities, other). Don't invent amounts the user
   didn't give.

3. If asked for an audit/summary, total spending by category and to date, then
   estimate harvest value: what was/will be harvested, at roughly what it would cost
   to buy the equivalent at a store or farmers market. Use WebSearch for current
   produce pricing rather than assuming stale figures.

4. Give a plain financial read — net positive/negative, and whether recurring costs
   (tools, infrastructure) are one-time investments that pay off over multiple
   seasons versus truly sunk cost. Don't sugarcoat a bad ROI just to be encouraging.

5. Write or update `Gardening - Cost Log.md` with the new entries and/or the latest
   audit summary. Tag `#gardening`. Link it under the "Costs" label in [[Gardening]].
