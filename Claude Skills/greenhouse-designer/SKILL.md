---
name: greenhouse-designer
description: >
  Design a greenhouse's physical layout — footprint/dimensions, structure type
  (lean-to, freestanding, hoop house), and framing/glazing material choices.
  Trigger on "design a greenhouse," "help me plan my greenhouse layout," or "what
  materials should I use for a greenhouse." Exploratory/reference planning only —
  not a build schedule (see greenhouse-builder) or a cost breakdown (see
  greenhouse-price-optimizer).
---

1. Check [[Greenhouse]] for existing design notes linked under its "Dimensions" and
   "Materials" labels before starting — build on what's there rather than re-deriving.

2. Clarify with the user if not already given: available space/footprint, primary use
   (seed starting vs year-round growing vs specific crops), climate/region, and any
   structural constraints (existing wall to lean against, HOA rules, etc).

3. Propose 2–3 structure options (lean-to, freestanding, hoop house, etc.) with
   tradeoffs, and a materials shortlist for framing and glazing (e.g. polycarbonate vs
   glass vs poly film) suited to the stated use and climate. Use WebSearch for
   specifics you're not confident on from general knowledge (typical R-values,
   durability, climate suitability) rather than asserting.

4. Write or update a dedicated note `Greenhouse Design.md`: footprint & dimensions,
   chosen/candidate structure type, framing & glazing materials, and open questions.
   Tag `#greenhouse`. Link it under both the "Dimensions" and "Materials" labels in
   [[Greenhouse]], matching that file's existing flat-list style.

5. Stay scoped to design. Don't produce a construction sequence (greenhouse-builder's
   job), a growing/operations plan (greenhouse-instructor's job), or firm pricing
   (greenhouse-price-optimizer's job) — note them as follow-ups instead.
