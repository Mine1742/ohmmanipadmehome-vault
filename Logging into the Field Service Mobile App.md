Logging into the Field Service Mobile App – Production:
1. Open the Field Service Mobile app.
2. Tap on the Gear icon (or 3 dots for Android) at the top right corner of the 
page and select “Production”.
3. Choose “Use Custom Domain”. <bottom>
4. Enter “Archkeysolutions.my.salesforce.com” in the Custom Domain field 
and tap Continue.
5. The option “Log in with Azure AD SSO” should appear, tap on it. <bottom>
6. Enter your Archkey login credentials, and you should access to the app upon 
successful login.

If you encounter any difficulties while logging into the Field Service App, 
please don't hesitate to reach out to us at Salesforce.Support@archkey.com 
for assistance.

Logging into the Field Service Mobile App – Sandbox (UAT):
1. Open the Field Service Mobile app.
2. Tap on the Gear icon (or 3 dots for Android) at the top right corner of the 
page and select “Sandbox”.
3. Enter your Sandbox (UAT) Username and Password.
4. Tap the “Log in to Sandbox” button to proceed.
