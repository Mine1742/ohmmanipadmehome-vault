# Dao of Life — Whistleblower & Non‑Retaliation Policy

> Dao of Life requires directors, officers, employees, volunteers, and contractors to observe high standards of business and personal ethics. This policy provides a process to report suspected violations and protects good‑faith reporters from retaliation.

---

## 1) Scope of Reportable Concerns
- Violations of law or regulations, fraud, theft, misuse of assets, conflicts of interest, unsafe practices, harassment or discrimination, retaliation, or substantial violations of policy or ethical standards.

## 2) Reporting Channels
- **Primary:** Chair of the Board (or designee) — [email/phone].  
- **Alternative:** Finance & Audit Committee Chair — [email/phone].  
- **Anonymous Option:** [describe method, e.g., third‑party hotline or dedicated email].

Reports should include as much detail as possible. If a concern implicates the Chair or Finance & Audit Chair, report to the **Secretary** or any two disinterested directors.

## 3) Investigation
- The Chair (or designee) will promptly acknowledge, log, and evaluate reports; assign an impartial investigator; and keep the Board (or the Finance & Audit Committee) informed as appropriate.
- Investigations will be conducted confidentially to the extent practicable and consistent with a thorough review.

## 4) Non‑Retaliation
- **No retaliation** against any person who, in **good faith**, reports a concern, participates in an investigation, or refuses to engage in suspected wrongdoing.
- Retaliation will result in discipline up to removal or termination.
- Knowingly false or bad‑faith reports may result in discipline.

## 5) Confidentiality
Dao of Life will protect the identity of reporters to the extent possible. Information will be shared strictly on a need‑to‑know basis.

## 6) Records & Reporting
The Secretary will maintain records of complaints, investigations, and outcomes, subject to the **Document Retention Policy**.
