## Chart of accounts (summary)
Condensed from the church's chart of accounts on file in Drive. Full numbered list lives in
`TheDao Socials.xlsx` (Drive) — that file also contains live credentials, so don't copy it
into this vault wholesale; only category summaries belong here.

- **Assets:** Operating checking (MD & MI), pledges/grants receivable, prepaid expenses, program-supplies inventory, land, buildings & improvements, furniture & equipment
- **Liabilities:** Accounts payable, accrued payroll, payroll taxes payable, deferred revenue
- **Equity:** Net assets — with/without donor restrictions
- **Income:** Individual & restricted contributions, foundation grants, DAF grants, program service revenue (workshops/fees), special events revenue, in-kind contributions, investment/interest income
- **Expenses:** Salaries, payroll taxes & benefits, professional/contracted services, garden supplies & seeds, food distribution supplies, occupancy, insurance, printing/advertising, postage, technology, travel/training, volunteer support (non-cash), depreciation, mutual aid grants, outreach & education, dues & subscriptions, bank & merchant fees

### Tracking dimensions (multi-branch)
Per the GAAP chart of accounts (Drive, "The_Dao_Incorp_Docs"), each transaction is tagged along three dimensions so branches and restrictions stay separable in reporting:
- **Class** (program function): `PG-Garden`, `PG-MutualAid`, `PG-Education`, `SUP-MgmtGeneral`, `SUP-Fundraising`
- **Location** (branch): `LOC-MD-HQ`, `LOC-MI-Branch`
- **Project/Grant** (restriction tracking): `GR-YYYY-XYZ`, `RSTR-Garden`, `RSTR-Education`, `TIME-Release-YYYY`
- Restricted contributions are split by purpose (Garden / Mutual Aid / Education / Capital-Facilities) rather than lumped into one restricted-income line.
- Worked example: a Michigan garden seed purchase = account 6310 + Class `PG-Garden` + Location `LOC-MI-Branch` + Project `RSTR-Garden` (if restricted).

### Donor acknowledgment
Seven ready-to-use letter templates exist in Drive (cash gift, quid-pro-quo/event ticket, non-cash property, in-kind professional services, grant/DAF distribution, recurring-donor year-end summary, restricted/purpose-specific gift). Legal reminders baked into them: written acknowledgment is required for any single gift ≥$250; quid-pro-quo benefit value must be disclosed for gifts >$75.
