# 📆 Granting Access to a SharePoint Calendar

Use this guide to share a calendar in SharePoint with team members or external users, whether you're working with a classic list-based calendar or a Microsoft 365 Group-integrated calendar.

---

## 🔹 Option 1: Classic SharePoint Calendar (List-based)

### ✅ Steps:

1. **Navigate to the calendar**:
   - Go to your SharePoint site
   - Click **Site Contents** > Open the calendar

2. **Open Calendar Settings**:
   - Click the **gear icon (⚙️)** in the upper right
   - Select **List settings**

3. **Manage Permissions**:
   - Scroll to **Permissions and Management**
   - Click **Permissions for this calendar**

4. **Stop Inheriting Permissions** (if needed):
   - Click **Stop Inheriting Permissions** to make calendar-specific changes

5. **Grant Access**:
   - Click **Grant Permissions**
   - Enter names or email addresses of users/groups
   - Select a permission level:
     - **Read** – view only
     - **Contribute** – add/edit events
     - **Full Control** – manage everything

6. Click **Share** or **OK**

---

## 🔹 Option 2: Modern SharePoint Calendar (M365 Group Calendar)

If your SharePoint calendar is linked to a Microsoft 365 Group, follow these steps.

### ✅ Steps:

1. **Open the Calendar in Outlook Web**:
   - Visit: [https://outlook.office.com/calendar/](https://outlook.office.com/calendar/)
   - Find the calendar under **Groups** in the sidebar

2. **Manage Group Members**:
   - Visit: [https://admin.microsoft.com](https://admin.microsoft.com)
   - Go to **Groups > Active groups**
   - Select the Microsoft 365 Group connected to your SharePoint site
   - Click **Members > View all and manage members**
   - Click **Add members**, enter names/emails

✅ These members now have access to:
- The Outlook calendar
- The associated SharePoint site and files

---

## 🧠 Tips & Best Practices

| Action | Use When |
|--------|----------|
| Add specific users | Granting limited or short-term access |
| Add to SharePoint security group | Standard for internal teams |
| Add to Microsoft 365 Group | Best for full collaboration (calendar + files + Teams) |
