# BYLAWS OF DAO OF LIFE [CHURCH/INC.]

> Replace bracketed choices to fit your governance (e.g., Church vs Inc.; with/without members). These bylaws contemplate a Maryland headquarters and branches, starting with Michigan.

---

## Article 1 — Name, Offices, Fiscal Year
**1.1 Name.** Dao of Life [Church/Inc.].  
**1.2 Principal Office.** Maryland; additional branches may operate in other states, beginning with Michigan.  
**1.3 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year ends **[Month Day]** each year.

## Article 2 — Mission & Values
**2.1 Mission.** Promote peace, harmony, solidarity among workers, inclusion, and climate‑positive living; end hunger and poverty via local gardening and equitable food systems; offer non‑dogmatic spiritual exploration and community.  
**2.2 Non‑Discrimination.** Inclusive of all; no discrimination based on race, color, national origin, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion/belief, union status, or any protected trait.  
**2.3 Prohibition on Private Benefit.** No private inurement; reasonable compensation allowed.

## Article 3 — Members (optional)
**3.1 Classes.** **[If applicable]** Members may be “Congregational Members” and “Service Volunteers,” as defined in policy.  
**3.2 Rights.** **[If applicable]** Voting rights (e.g., electing a portion of the Board) may be granted by policy.  
**3.3 No Dues Requirement.** Participation shall not be conditioned on donations.  
**3.4 No Members (alternative).** If operating without members: “The Corporation shall have no members; all corporate powers reside in the Board.”

## Article 4 — Board of Directors / Trustees (“Board”)
**4.1 Authority.** Governing body; sets policy; oversees assets, risk, and mission.  
**4.2 Composition.** **[3–15]** directors. Aim for at least **one‑third** from worker/union communities and meaningful representation of marginalized groups.  
**4.3 Terms.** **[e.g., 3‑year staggered terms],** maximum **[2]** consecutive terms.  
**4.4 Meetings & Quorum.** At least **quarterly**; quorum is **majority** of directors; electronic meetings allowed.  
**4.5 Voting.** Majority of those present unless a greater vote is required. No proxy voting.  
**4.6 Officers.** At minimum: Chair, Secretary, Treasurer; elected annually by the Board.  
**4.7 Committees.** Executive, Finance & Audit, Programs (Gardening & Food Equity), Governance & Nominating, Ethics & Safeguarding.  
**4.8 Removal/Vacancies.** For cause or without cause by **[two‑thirds]** vote; Board fills vacancies.  
**4.9 Compensation.** Directors are uncompensated; reimbursement of reasonable expenses allowed.  
**4.10 Conflicts.** See separate **Conflict of Interest Policy** (incorporated by reference).

## Article 5 — Spiritual/Program Leadership
**5.1 Clergy/Facilitators.** The Board may appoint spiritual facilitators or ministers; roles are non‑dogmatic and inclusive.  
**5.2 Housing Allowance.** The Board may designate clergy housing allowances by resolution annually (for tax purposes).

## Article 6 — Chapters & Branches
**6.1 Authorization.** The Board may establish **branches/chapters**, including the **Michigan Branch**, subject to these Bylaws and Board policies.  
**6.2 Local Councils.** Branches may have Local Councils for operations, subject to Board oversight.  
**6.3 Funds.** Branch funds are organizational assets; branch accounts must follow central internal controls and accounting.  
**6.4 Compliance.** Branches must comply with host‑state registration, reporting, and employment laws; the central office will maintain registrations for foreign qualification as needed.

## Article 7 — Compliance & Advocacy Guardrails
**7.1 501(c)(3) Compliance.** Strictly nonpartisan; no candidate endorsements or campaign intervention.  
**7.2 Policy Advocacy.** Issue advocacy consistent with mission is permitted within legal limits; the Board may elect the **§501(h)** lobbying expenditure test.  
**7.3 Related Entities.** If broader advocacy/electoral work is desired, it must occur via separate entities (e.g., §501(c)(4) or PAC) with arms‑length controls and cost‑sharing agreements.

## Article 8 — Finance & Records
**8.1 Accounting.** U.S. GAAP for nonprofits; two net asset classes (with/without donor restrictions).  
**8.2 Budgets & Authority.** Annual budget approved by the Board; dual‑signature disbursements; spending authority by policy.  
**8.3 Audits/Reviews.** External CPA **[audit/review/compilation]** as determined by the Board and applicable thresholds.  
**8.4 Records.** Accurate minutes, policies, and financials maintained; reasonable public disclosure as required by law.  
**8.5 UBI.** Activities unrelated to exempt purposes shall be tracked; file Form 990‑T if required.

## Article 9 — Indemnification & Insurance
**9.1 Indemnification.** To the fullest extent permitted by Maryland law, the Corporation shall indemnify its directors, trustees, officers, employees, and authorized agents.  
**9.2 Insurance.** The Corporation shall maintain Directors & Officers liability, general liability, and other insurance (including workers’ compensation and property, as applicable) as determined by the Board.

## Article 10 — Safeguarding & Ethics
**10.1 Safeguarding.** Policies shall address safety in programs (including work with minors and vulnerable adults), mandatory reporting, background checks as appropriate, and respectful conduct.  
**10.2 Whistleblower & Document Retention.** The Corporation shall maintain and enforce **Whistleblower** and **Document Retention & Destruction** policies.

## Article 11 — Amendments
These Bylaws may be amended by a **[two‑thirds]** vote of the Board at any regular or special meeting, provided at least **[10]** days’ notice of the proposed amendment is given.

## Article 12 — Adoption
These Bylaws were adopted by the Board of **Dao of Life [Church/Inc.]** on **[Date]** and supersede any prior bylaws.
