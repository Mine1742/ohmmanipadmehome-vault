# How to Add BCC in Outlook

BCC (**Blind Carbon Copy**) allows you to send an email where recipients in this field are hidden from other recipients.

---

## 🔍 Outlook for Windows or Mac Desktop App

### Steps:
1. Open Outlook and start a new email (**Home > New Email**).
2. In the new message window, click on the **"Options"** tab.
3. Select **"BCC"** to show the BCC field.
4. Add email addresses in the **BCC** field.

[Microsoft Support: Add Bcc recipients in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/add-bcc-recipients-in-outlook-6e07c6b9-1f3e-4a6a-bb14-51f8aab0f64a)

---

## 🔍 Outlook on the Web (Outlook.com / Office 365)

### Steps:
1. Click **"New mail"**.
2. Click on the **"..." (More actions)** button in the message window.
3. Select **"Show Bcc"**.
4. Enter addresses in the **BCC** field.

[Microsoft Support: Send an email in Outlook on the web](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/send-an-email-message-in-outlook-on-the-web-76d8c5c0-18f3-4f52-8962-22fa84179b99)

---

## 🔍 Outlook Mobile App (iOS/Android)

### Steps:
1. Tap the **Compose** icon to start a new message.
2. Tap **"Cc/Bcc"** below the "To" field.
3. Enter addresses in the **BCC** section.

---

## 🔍 Key Notes About BCC
- **Privacy:** Other recipients won't see who is in the BCC field.
- **Group emails:** Helps avoid exposing email addresses in large distributions.
- **Confidentiality:** Useful when sending emails without revealing recipients' identities.
