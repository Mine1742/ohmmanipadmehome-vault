
# Outlook on iPhone – How to Use the Global Address List (GAL)

> The GAL isn’t something you “install” on the phone. Outlook already knows how to search your organization’s **Directory** (GAL). You can also save specific directory entries to your iPhone’s Contacts if needed.

---

## Option A — Use GAL in the Outlook app (recommended)

1) Open **Outlook** (blue “O”). Make sure you’re signed in with your work account.  
2) Tap the **Search** icon (magnifying glass) at the bottom.  
3) In the search bar, start typing the person’s **name**, **email**, or **department**.  
4) If you don’t see them under Contacts, tap **Search Directory** (sometimes shown as **Search organization**).  
5) Tap the result to view their card.  
6) (Optional) Tap **Add to contacts** to save this person in your Outlook contacts.

> Tip: This searches the full company **Global Address List (GAL)** even if you haven’t saved the person before.

---

## Option B — Make GAL people show up in the iPhone Contacts app (selected people only)

You can’t sync the entire GAL to the phone, but you can save people you look up to the local Contacts so they show in Phone/FaceTime/iMessage.

1) In Outlook, tap your **avatar → Settings (gear)**.  
2) Tap your **work account**.  
3) Turn on **Save Contacts** (allow access when prompted).  
4) When you view a person from the Directory, tap **Add to contacts**.  
   - That person now appears in **iOS Contacts** and caller ID.

> Note: Policies set by your IT admin may disable **Save Contacts**.

---

## Option C — Search the GAL via the native iOS Contacts/Mail apps

If your org prefers the built‑in iOS apps:

1) On iPhone go to **Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Microsoft Exchange**.  
2) Sign in with your work account and **enable Contacts**.  
3) Open the **Contacts** app and use the search bar. At the bottom of results, tap **Search Directory** (or **Company Directory**).  
4) Tap a result → **Add to Existing Contact** or **Create New Contact** if you want it saved locally.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Can’t find “Search Directory” in Outlook?** Update Outlook from the App Store and confirm you’re signed into your work account.  
- **No “Save Contacts” toggle?** Your organization may disable it via policy. Use Option C to search when dialing from the Phone app.  
- **Still no results?** Verify network/VPN, and that your mailbox is **Exchange/Entra ID**–backed (not POP/IMAP).

---

## Quick facts

- The **full GAL does not fully sync** to iPhone. You **search** it and **save individuals** you need.  
- Saved contacts will continue to update only if Outlook policies allow it; otherwise, re-save as needed.
