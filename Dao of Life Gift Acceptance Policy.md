# Dao of Life — Gift Acceptance Policy

> Purpose: to ensure that all gifts to Dao of Life (the “Organization”) are structured to further its religious, charitable, and educational purposes; to protect donors and the Organization; and to maintain compliance with applicable law and ethical standards.

---

## 1) General Principles
- Gifts must support the mission and **not** compromise independence, public trust, or program integrity.
- Donor intent will be honored; **restricted** gifts must be usable for the specified purpose and administratively feasible.
- The Organization does **not provide tax or legal advice**. Donors should consult their advisors.
- Acknowledgments and receipts will follow IRS and state rules.

## 2) Authority & Oversight
- The **Executive Director (or Chair)** may accept routine gifts consistent with this policy.  
- The **Board (or Gift Review Committee)** must pre‑approve **non‑routine** or **complex** gifts, including:
  - real estate; closely held business interests; tangible property > $5,000; vehicles; artwork/collectibles; **cryptoassets**; gifts with ongoing obligations; restricted endowments; naming rights; gifts that could trigger **UBIT**; and any gift with **conditions** or reputational risk.

## 3) Acceptable Gift Types
- **Cash/Checks/EFT/Credit‑Card/Online.**
- **Publicly Traded Securities.** Sold as soon as practicable after receipt unless the Finance policy provides otherwise.
- **DAF Grants / Private Foundation Grants.**
- **Corporate Matching Gifts and Sponsorships** (content remains independent; no endorsement implied).
- **In‑Kind Goods/Services** that further programs (e.g., tools, supplies, pro bono professional services). In‑kind services are acknowledged with a description only (no valuation by the Organization).
- **Bequests & Beneficiary Designations** (wills, trusts, retirement plans, life insurance).
- **Cryptoassets** via an approved third‑party processor or exchange; generally liquidate immediately into USD.

## 4) Gifts Requiring Special Review
- **Tangible Personal Property**: accept only if (a) mission‑useful or (b) readily marketable. Donor responsible for IRS **Form 8283** appraisals when required; Organization may file **Form 8282** if disposed within 3 years.
- **Real Estate**: require environmental review, marketability analysis, title review, and Board approval. Donor pays carrying costs until transfer.
- **Closely Held Business Interests/LLC Units/Partnerships**: require due diligence (UBIT, transfer restrictions, valuation).
- **Restricted or Conditional Gifts**: must be documented by a **Gift Agreement**; no restrictions that unlawfully discriminate or unduly limit organizational speech or control.
- **Endowment/Quasi‑Endowment**: governed by **UPMIFA** principles; spending policy approved by the Board.

## 5) Donor Privacy & Recognition
- Donor information is kept confidential unless disclosure is required by law or authorized by the donor. Anonymous gifts will be honored.
- Recognition and naming opportunities require Board approval and may be revoked if continuing recognition would harm the Organization’s mission or reputation.

## 6) Acknowledgment & Valuation
- Written acknowledgments will include the amount (or description of non‑cash gifts), date, and a statement whether goods/services were provided in exchange. For quid‑pro‑quo gifts > $75, the fair market value of benefits received will be disclosed.
- The Organization does not appraise gifts; donors are responsible for valuations.

## 7) Refusal of Gifts
The Organization may decline any gift that: (a) is inconsistent with mission or policies; (b) would create unacceptable liability or expense; (c) carries restrictions that cannot be honored; or (d) could damage reputation or independence.
