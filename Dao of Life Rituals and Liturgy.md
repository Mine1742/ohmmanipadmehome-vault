## Regular service structure
1. **Opening Rituals** — lighting a candle, planting a symbolic seed, or ringing a bell; recitation of the Creed.
2. **Teachings and Readings** — readings from the Dao of Life Handbook or related texts; reflections on peace/justice/sustainability/solidarity; community discussion.
3. **Meditation and Solidarity Practices** — silent meditation or guided visualization; breathing practices and group affirmations; optional music, chants, or drumming.
4. **Acts of Worship in Service** — communal service framed as sacred (gardening, food prep, outreach); symbolic acts of solidarity (letters of support, banners for worker causes).
5. **Closing Rituals** — creed/call-and-response affirmation ("We walk in peace." / "We live in solidarity." / "We honor the Earth."), candle extinguished, moment of silence or circle closing.

**Example weekly service timing:** Opening (5 min) → Reading & Reflection (15 min) → Meditation (10 min) → Service Action (20 min) → Sharing (10 min) → Closing (5 min).

## The four seasonal rituals
| Ritual | Season | Symbol | Activity | Reflection theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planting Ritual | Spring | Seeds, soil, water | Collective planting, affirmations of growth/renewal | Hope, labor, new beginnings |
| Summer Justice Gathering | Summer | Sun, tools, banners | Outdoor service projects, demonstrations, climate-positive actions | Strength, justice, unity |
| Harvest Ritual | Autumn | Basket of food / first fruits | Sharing meals, distributing produce to community | Gratitude, solidarity, abundance |
| Winter Reflection Gathering | Winter | Candle/firelight in the dark | Silent reflection, storytelling, honoring ancestors and past struggles | Rest, resilience, endurance |

Each seasonal service runs the standard five-part structure with the "Acts of Worship" slot swapped for the season's Sacred Action. Common tagline: "All are welcome • Bring a friend • Join us in service and fellowship."

## Ministerial role (Guides / Ordained Ministers)
- Lead services, reflections, and rituals; ensure inclusivity/accessibility/clarity; provide spiritual counsel and education.
- **Training path:** study the Dao of Life Handbook and philosophy → complete service projects as training → lead trial services under mentorship → ordination ceremony.
- **Ongoing responsibilities:** facilitate gatherings, maintain ritual consistency, mentor new members, safeguard the integrity of practices.

## Special (non-seasonal) services
- **Solidarity Vigils** — during strikes, disasters, climate emergencies.
- **Community Celebrations** — garden launches, new branch openings, anniversaries.
- **Memorials** — honoring members, workers, or the deceased.
- **Dedications** — blessings of new gardens, homes, or community projects.

## Music/chants
Short affirmations used in services: "Peace, harmony, solidarity." and "We honor Earth, we honor life." Optional drumming, singing, chanting. Readings draw from the Dao of Life Handbook, labor/justice/nature poetry, and texts from global traditions affirming compassion and sustainability.
