## Legal structure
- **Entity:** "The Dao Of Life" — Maryland religious corporation, incorporated via MD SDAT Articles of Incorporation for Religious Corporations.
- **Purpose (Article Third):** organized exclusively for religious, charitable, and educational purposes under IRC 501(c)(3); advances peace, inclusion, worker solidarity, climate-positive living, hunger relief, and sustainable food systems through worship/assembly, spiritual teaching, education, mutual aid, and community programs including local gardening.
- **EIN:** assigned by the IRS (confirmation letter on file). EIN itself intentionally not reproduced here — treat it like any other sensitive ID.
- **501(c)(3) recognition — open item:** an EIN alone does not grant tax-exempt recognition (per IRS Pub 1828). Verify whether Form 1023 / 1023-EZ has actually been filed and approved, separate from the EIN assignment.
- Source docs live in Drive folder "The Dao Of Life Docs": `MD incorp form.pdf`, `DaoEIN.pdf`, `IRS_Guidance_p1828.pdf`.

## Bylaws
**Status: drafted template, not yet Board-adopted.** Source: `Dao_of_Life_Bylaws_Template.md` (Drive, "The_Dao_Incorp_Docs").
- Maryland HQ plus a named **Michigan Branch**; Board can establish further branches/Local Councils, subject to central accounting controls and host-state law compliance.
- Board: 3–15 directors/trustees, staggered terms (e.g. 3-year, max 2 consecutive — adjustable), aspirational goal of at least one-third from worker/union and marginalized-group representation.
- Officers: minimum Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, elected annually by the Board. Committees: Executive; Finance & Audit; Programs (Gardening & Food Equity); Governance & Nominating; Ethics & Safeguarding.
- Meets at least quarterly; quorum = majority of directors; electronic meetings allowed; no proxy voting; ordinary votes by majority present.
- Directors uncompensated (expense reimbursement only); full indemnification under MD law; D&O/general liability/workers' comp/property insurance as the Board determines.
- Board may appoint non-dogmatic spiritual facilitators/ministers and designate a clergy housing allowance by resolution.
- 501(c)(3) guardrails: strictly nonpartisan, no candidate endorsement; issue advocacy permitted; Board may elect the §501(h) lobbying expenditure test; any electoral/broader advocacy work routes through a separate related entity (e.g. a 501(c)(4) or PAC).
- Safeguarding provisions for minors/vulnerable adults, mandatory reporting, background checks — cross-references the Whistleblower and Document Retention policies below.
- Amendment: two-thirds Board vote with advance notice.

## Core policies
**Status: drafted, awaiting Board adoption** (see Initial Board Resolutions below). Source: 4 policy docs + a disclosure form in Drive, "The_Dao_Incorp_Docs".
- **Conflict of Interest** — covers directors/officers/key employees plus family members and related organizations. Disclosure to the Chair and Secretary before the org acts; recusal from deliberation/vote; approval requires a majority of disinterested directors; annual signed disclosure form required of every covered person.
- **Whistleblower & Non-Retaliation** — report to the Board Chair or the Finance & Audit Committee Chair (or the Secretary/two disinterested directors if the Chair is implicated); anonymous reporting option; non-retaliation guaranteed for good-faith reports; Secretary maintains records per the retention policy.
- **Document Retention & Destruction** — Secretary is the designated Records Officer. Permanent: Articles, Bylaws, IRS determination letter/Form 1023, minutes, major policies, audits, deeds, key contracts. 10 yr: major contracts, incident reports, insurance policies. 7 yr: general ledgers, financial statements, payroll, Form 990-T, donor/contribution records. 4 yr: routine correspondence, volunteer files. 1–3 yr: internal notes/drafts. Litigation holds override the schedule.
- **Gift Acceptance** — accepts cash, publicly traded securities (sold promptly), DAF/foundation grants, in-kind goods/services, bequests, and cryptoassets (liquidated immediately via an approved processor). Requires Board pre-approval for real estate, closely-held business interests, tangible property >$5,000, vehicles, art/collectibles, restricted endowments, naming rights, or anything triggering UBIT. Will refuse gifts that conflict with the mission or create unacceptable liability/reputational risk.
