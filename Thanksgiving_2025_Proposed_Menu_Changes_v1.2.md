RATIONALE & PER-COURSE DETAILS
- Why these changes: one or two simple sauce additions create distinct ways to eat the same dish (e.g., turkey with chimichurri vs. turkey with classic gravy). Textural additions (crunchy panko, nuts, crisp shallots) balance fat and cream.

- Turkey & Compound Butter: keep your brine and compound butter. Add a "smoked orange brown-butter" or a "bright herb chimichurri" and provide both at the table. Rationale: guests can decide if they want a classic holiday profile or brighter herb acidity.

- Potato Courses: additions create textural contrast. The scalloped gets a crunchy top, the mash gets an umami drizzle.

- Veg Courses: small flavor finishes (preserved lemon, sumac, pepita crisps, Aleppo) expand the palate across the buffet and create a thread of flavors while using minimal extra ingredients.

- Desserts: minor dark-chocolate/espresso/orange touches or a pepita-brown-butter garnish can make the desserts taste more composed and memorable, while using ingredients in other dishes (pepitas, brown butter).

CORE FINISHES & CONDIMENTS (3 Recipes - quick)

1) Herb Chimichurri (Bright finishing herb oil)
- Use: finish turkey, potatoes, cauliflower
- Qty: makes ~1½ cups
Ingredients:
- 1 cup flat-leaf parsley (packed)
- 1 cup packed cilantro (optional; keep parsley if you want more traditional)
- 1/4 cup fresh chives
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar (or sherry vinegar)
- Zest & juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper (opt)
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil

Method: Blitz parsley, chives, garlic, vinegar, lemon & salt in a food processor; stream olive oil until emulsion forms. Finish with zest; adjust acidity.

2) Smoked-Orange Brown Butter
- Use: finish carrots & sweet potato pavé; dot over turkey slices
- Qty: makes ~1 stick brown butter
Ingredients:
- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
- Zest of 1 orange
- 1/2 tsp smoked sea salt (or smoked paprika + small salt)

Method: Brown the butter until toasty and aromatic; remove from heat. Stir in orange zest and smoked salt. Keep in small jars—tiny spoon at the table.

3) Black Garlic–Miso Glaze (a touch of umami)
- Use: for pan gravy, to finish mushrooms & boiled veg
- Qty: 1/2 cup
Ingredients:
- 3 tbsp white miso
- 3–4 cloves black garlic (mashed)
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 1–2 tbsp warm stock to thin

Method: Whisk miso, black garlic, maple, vinegar & thin with stock. Brush on end-of-roast or add a tablespoon to pan gravy.
