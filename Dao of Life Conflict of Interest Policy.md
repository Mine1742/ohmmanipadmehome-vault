# Dao of Life — Conflict of Interest Policy

> Purpose: to protect Dao of Life's interests when it contemplates a transaction or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of a director, trustee, officer, key employee, or related party. This policy is intended to supplement (not replace) applicable law.

---

## 1) Covered Persons
This policy applies to directors/trustees, officers, committee members with Board-delegated powers, and **key employees** (collectively, “Covered Persons”). It also applies when a Covered Person’s **Family Member** or **Related Organization** has an interest.

- **Family Member:** spouse or domestic partner, ancestors, siblings (whole or half), children, grandchildren, great‑grandchildren, and the spouses of siblings, children, grandchildren, or great‑grandchildren.
- **Related Organization:** an entity in which the Covered Person or Family Member has a material financial interest or serves as director, officer, or employee.

## 2) Conflict of Interest
A conflict exists when a Covered Person has a **Financial Interest** or other personal interest that may compromise, or appear to compromise, the person’s independent judgment in Dao of Life’s best interests.

- **Financial Interest** includes: ownership or investment interest; compensation arrangements (direct or indirect); prospective employment; or any benefit from a transaction with Dao of Life.

## 3) Duty to Disclose
A Covered Person must **disclose** the existence and all material facts of any actual or potential conflict **before** Dao of Life acts on the matter and **recuse** from related deliberations and votes unless the Board (or committee) requests factual input.

Disclosures shall be made to the **Chair** (or committee chair) and **Secretary** and recorded in minutes.

## 4) Procedures for Addressing Conflicts
1. The **Chair** (or disinterested committee) determines whether a conflict exists after disclosure.
2. The disinterested directors/committee shall, if appropriate, appoint a non‑interested person to investigate alternatives.
3. The Board/committee shall approve the transaction only if it determines it is **fair and reasonable** to Dao of Life and in its **best interests**.
4. Approval requires a **majority vote of disinterested directors** present, with quorum composed of disinterested directors.
5. The interested Covered Person shall **not** be present for discussion or vote (except to provide requested factual information).

## 5) Compensation & Independent Approval
Voting on one’s own **compensation** is not permitted. Compensation for directors, officers, and key employees shall be approved in advance by disinterested directors using appropriate **comparability data** and documented in minutes.

## 6) Records of Proceedings
Minutes shall reflect: (a) names of persons with conflicts; (b) the nature of the conflict; (c) content of discussion, including alternatives; (d) the vote; and (e) any recusals.

## 7) Annual Statements
Each Covered Person shall **annually sign** the attached **Conflict of Interest Disclosure** affirming they:
- received and read this policy,
- agree to comply with it, and
- understand Dao of Life’s charitable, religious, and educational purposes and that it is tax‑exempt.

## 8) Periodic Reviews
The Board (or Finance & Audit Committee) shall conduct periodic reviews to ensure ongoing compliance, including reasonableness of compensation and that arrangements further Dao of Life’s purposes and do not result in private inurement or excess benefit transactions.

## 9) Use of Outside Experts
When conducting reviews, Dao of Life may use outside advisors. Use of experts does not replace the Board’s responsibility.
