to synch the old emails from the imap server go to the send/receive tab and update the folder to force the synch

if folders dont synch straight away:
- In Outlook Classic, go to File > Account Settings > Account Settings.
- Select your account, click "Change," then "More Settings," and go to the "Advanced" tab.
- Ensure the "Download shared folders" option is checked. Try unchecking it, restarting Outlook, and then re-checking it.
