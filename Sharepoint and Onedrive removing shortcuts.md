
SharePoint shortcuts into OneDrive. Do not MOVE or DELETE these shortcuts. Doing so will delete the content from SharePoint. If you are fixing a OneDrive issue and you need to remove a link you can do it one of two ways.

Web - Right click shortcut within their one drive and Click "Remove"
![[Pasted image 20250212142956.jpg]]

File Explorer - Navigate to their OneDrive, right click the link, click on "Remove Shortcut"

![[Pasted image 20250212143038.jpg]]

Both processes will require their OneDrive to process the changes.

If you need to reset OneDrive, this will resync their entire OneDrive
