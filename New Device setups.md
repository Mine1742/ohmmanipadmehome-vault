#Mona 

For mobile devices, the device naming convention is as follows:

Site Code-Last 6 of SN-Device Code-Device Year
Site code (MEG) - last 6 digits of serial number (6NFPHW) - Device Code: Standard Tablet (ST) - Year (25) Standard Phone (SP) For laptops it is AKS-Serial Number
