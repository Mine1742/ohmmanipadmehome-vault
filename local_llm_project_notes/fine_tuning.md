# Fine-Tuning Your Local LLM

## What is Fine-Tuning?
Fine-tuning adjusts a pre-trained LLM on your specific dataset to specialize it for your tasks (e.g., helpdesk answers, coding style, document Q&A).

## Options for Fine-Tuning on CPU

| Method | Tools | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) | Hugging Face PEFT | Light and efficient |
| Full fine-tuning | Hugging Face Transformers | Not recommended on low-RAM systems |
