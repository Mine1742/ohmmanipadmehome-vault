---
name: solar-conceptualizer
description: >
  Early-stage solar power brainstorming — energy needs assessment, feasibility, and
  high-level options (grid-tied vs off-grid, roof vs ground mount, battery vs no
  battery). Trigger on "I'm thinking about solar power," "is solar worth it for me,"
  or "what are my solar options." First stop before solar-designer/solar-architect,
  which need a concept settled to go into detail.
---

1. Check [[Solar Power]]'s "Concepts" section for existing notes before starting fresh.

2. Clarify with the user if not already given: what the power is for (whole home,
   workshop, greenhouse/outbuilding, RV/off-grid cabin), rough energy usage or the
   loads to cover, location/climate (affects sun-hours), and budget ballpark.

3. Lay out the high-level decision points and options plainly, with tradeoffs — not a
   final design: grid-tied (net metering) vs off-grid vs hybrid; roof vs ground mount;
   battery storage vs none; DIY vs professional install. Use WebSearch for anything
   jurisdiction- or incentive-dependent you're not confident on (net metering rules,
   permitting norms) rather than asserting.

4. Give a rough feasibility read — is this a reasonable project given the stated needs
   and constraints, or are there red flags (shading, roof condition, budget mismatch)
   worth surfacing now before more detailed design work.

5. Write or update `Solar Power - Concept.md`: use case, constraints, the option(s)
   leaning toward, and open questions. Tag `#solar`. Link it under the "Concepts"
   label in [[Solar Power]].

6. Hand off, don't duplicate: once a concept is settled, point the user to
   solar-architect (site/physical layout) and solar-designer (electrical system sizing)
   for the next level of detail rather than designing it yourself here.
