---
name: venture-search-log
description: >
  Log a reflection, pivot, or decision in the entrepreneurial search process itself —
  separate from any single idea's card. Trigger on requests like "log my progress on
  the venture search," "I've decided to shelve X because Y," or "note this week's
  entrepreneur reflection."
---

1. Append a dated entry to [[Venture Search Log]]: what happened, what was decided (if
   anything), and why. This is about the search process as a whole (patterns across
   ideas, what you're learning about what you want, criteria that are emerging) — not
   a substitute for updating an individual idea's card in [[App Ideas]], which should
   still be updated directly if its status changed.

2. Don't invent reasoning the user didn't give — if they just say "shelving X," ask why
   before logging it as a decision with rationale.

3. Append-only — don't rewrite past entries.
