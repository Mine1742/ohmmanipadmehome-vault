---
name: entrepreneur-business-plan
description: >
  Generate or expand a business plan draft, or brainstorm business-model inspiration,
  for any business — existing or hypothetical. Trigger on "write a business plan for
  X," "help me think through my business model," or "give me inspiration for how to
  structure this business." Different from venture-ideate/venture-validate, which
  search for and vet new venture ideas — this drafts the plan for a business the user
  already has in mind.
---

1. Identify which business this is for and check for an existing
   `<Business Name> - Business Plan.md` note before starting fresh. If there's a
   matching deep-dive under [[App Ideas]] (a validated venture idea), read it first
   and build on that research rather than re-deriving it.

2. Clarify with the user if not already given: the core offering, target customer,
   and what stage this plan is for (raising money, personal clarity, applying for a
   loan/grant).

3. Draft or expand the plan with standard sections suited to the stated purpose:
   executive summary, problem/solution, target market, business model & pricing,
   competitive landscape, operations plan, and financial projections (high-level,
   defer detailed statements to entrepreneur-accountant). Use WebSearch for
   market/competitor facts you're not confident on rather than inventing figures.

4. For business-model inspiration requests (not a full plan), offer 2–3 concrete
   structural options (subscription vs one-time, services vs product, etc.) with
   tradeoffs rather than one generic answer.

5. Write or update `<Business Name> - Business Plan.md` with the drafted/expanded
   content. Tag `#entrepreneur`. Link it from [[Entrepreneur Hub]] if not already
   linked.
