---
name: life-goal
description: >
  Add, update, or close out a personal life goal (career, health, financial, hobby,
  etc.) in the Goals note. Trigger on requests like "add a goal to get my HAM radio
  license," "update my goal on X," or "I finished this goal."
---

1. Add or update a card in [[Goals]]: Area, Objective, Success criteria, Status
   (Active/Paused/Done/Abandoned), Target date, Current state. Ask for whatever the
   user didn't supply rather than inventing it.

2. When a goal's status changes, update Current state with what actually happened —
   don't just flip the status flag silently.

3. Don't duplicate a goal that's really a venture idea ([[App Ideas]]) or a Dao of Life
   governance item ([[Dao of Life Governance]]) — those have their own tracking. This
   note is for the user's own personal life goals.
