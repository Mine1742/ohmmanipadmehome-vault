---
name: life-task
description: >
  Add, complete, or list personal to-dos/errands — separate from project-specific
  action items already tracked elsewhere (Dao of Life meeting action items, venture
  next-steps). Trigger on requests like "add a todo," "remind me to X," "what's on my
  list," or "mark X done."
---

1. Add new items as `- [ ] <task>` under the right section in [[Personal Tasks]] —
   "Now / this week" for anything time-sensitive, "Later" for non-urgent, "Someday" for
   maybe-eventually. Ask which bucket if it's not obvious.

2. To complete a task, find the matching line and check it off (`- [x]`) rather than
   deleting it — leave completed items visible until the user asks to clear them out.

3. To list tasks, just read [[Personal Tasks]] back rather than reconstructing it from
   memory.

4. Don't add project-specific action items here — a Dao of Life meeting action item
   belongs in that meeting's note, a venture next-step belongs on its [[App Ideas]]
   card. This note is for the user's own personal to-dos only.
