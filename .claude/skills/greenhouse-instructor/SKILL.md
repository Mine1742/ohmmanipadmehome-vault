---
name: greenhouse-instructor
description: >
  Teach greenhouse growing/operation techniques — climate control, irrigation
  scheduling, crop selection, pest management. Trigger on "how do I grow X in my
  greenhouse," "teach me about greenhouse climate control," or "what should I plant
  this season." Different from greenhouse-designer/builder, which cover the structure
  itself, not what happens inside it.
---

1. Check [[Greenhouse]]'s "Irrigation" label and `Greenhouse Growing Guide.md` (if it
   exists) for prior notes before answering — build on established context (structure
   type and climate from `Greenhouse Design.md`) rather than answering generically.

2. Answer the specific question directly and thoroughly first — crop-specific
   requirements (temperature, humidity, light), irrigation scheduling, ventilation/
   climate control approach, or pest/disease management, as asked. Use WebSearch for
   anything region- or crop-specific you're not confident on (frost dates, cultivar
   requirements) rather than guessing.

3. Teach the underlying concept, not just the answer — briefly explain the "why" (e.g.
   why humidity control matters for a given crop) so the knowledge generalizes to the
   next question.

4. Write or update `Greenhouse Growing Guide.md` with reusable takeaways (climate
   targets, irrigation schedule, crop notes) — not a transcript of the conversation.
   Tag `#greenhouse`. Link it under the "Irrigation" label in [[Greenhouse]] (add an
   "Operation & Growing" label too if the content doesn't fit under Irrigation alone).

5. Stay scoped to growing/operating the greenhouse. Structural changes belong to
   greenhouse-designer/builder; cost questions belong to greenhouse-price-optimizer.
