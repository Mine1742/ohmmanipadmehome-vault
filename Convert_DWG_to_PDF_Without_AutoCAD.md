# 📄 Convert .DWG to .PDF Without AutoCAD or Revit

If you don't have access to AutoCAD or Revit, you can still convert `.dwg` files to `.pdf` using these alternative methods:

---

## 🧰 Option 1: Autodesk DWG TrueView (Free)

**DWG TrueView** is Autodesk's free viewer that also supports PDF plotting.

### ✅ Steps:
1. Download: [https://www.autodesk.com/viewers](https://www.autodesk.com/viewers)
2. Open your `.dwg` file in DWG TrueView.
3. Click **Plot** or type `PLOT`.
4. Set printer to `DWG to PDF.pc3`.
5. Choose your paper size, plot area, and scale.
6. Click **OK** and save the PDF file.

---

## 🌐 Option 2: Use an Online DWG to PDF Converter

For quick, no-install solutions:

- [CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/dwg-to-pdf)
- [AnyConv](https://anyconv.com/dwg-to-pdf-converter/)
- [Zamzar](https://www.zamzar.com/convert/dwg-to-pdf/)

### ⚠️ Security Note:
Only use online tools for non-sensitive files. Avoid uploading proprietary or confidential drawings.

---

## 🖥 Option 3: LibreCAD (Open Source)

LibreCAD can open and export older `.dwg` files.

### ✅ Steps:
1. Download: [https://librecad.org/](https://librecad.org/)
2. Open the `.dwg` file.
3. Go to `File → Export → PDF` or `Print` and select a PDF printer.

### ⚠️ Limitation:
Only supports 2D drawings and older `.dwg` formats (e.g., AutoCAD R14). Complex or newer files may not load properly.
