### Filing guide excerpt (Maryland SDAT)

FIRST: Insert the names and addresses of at least four adult persons who have been elected by the congregation to create this corporation. The only requirement is that they are at least 18 years old. The address should be one where mail can be received. It can be anywhere.

SECOND: Insert the name of the corporation you will have. It must be distinguishable from all other corporations on record in Maryland. To check name availability, you may conduct an online search at www.dat.maryland.gov, or call (410) 767-1330.

THIRD: Give a one or two sentence description of the purpose for which the corporation is being created. The description must include the forming of a Congregation or place of worship.

FOURTH: The trustees are the people who run this corporation, so the elections which name or replace them are very important. Any rule governing the election of trustees must be in the charter; this cannot be regulated by bylaws.

FIFTH: Insert the address of the principal place of worship — must have a zip code; a post office box is not accepted unless a street address is also provided.

SIXTH: Name and address of the individual designated to accept service of process — must be an adult citizen of Maryland or an existing Maryland corporation, address must be in Maryland (not a PO box), and this person must sign.

SEVENTH: 501(c)(3) language.

EIGHTH: All individuals listed in FIRST and the resident agent in SIXTH must sign.

**Filing instructions:** Online via Maryland Business Express (egov.maryland.gov/businessexpress), by mail to State Department of Assessments and Taxation, Charter Division, 700 East Pratt Street, Suite 2700, Baltimore, MD 21202, or by hand-delivery to the drop boxes at 123 Market Place, Baltimore, MD 21202.

**Processing time/fees:** Base filing $120 (6–8 weeks); expedited review +$50 (7–10 business days); same-day review +$325 online / +$425 in-person.

**Annual filing note:** all domestic and foreign legal entities must submit a Personal Property Return to the Department; failure results in forfeiture of the right to conduct business in Maryland. A separate exemption application exists for property owned by religious organizations (churches, parsonages, convents, educational buildings, church cemeteries) — must be received by September 1 for the current tax levy.
