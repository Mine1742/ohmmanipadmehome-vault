Troubleshooting External Monitor Issues

Check Connections:

Ensure the cables connecting your laptop to the external monitors are securely plugged in at both ends.
Try different ports on your laptop (e.g., HDMI, DisplayPort, VGA) if available.
If you're using adapters or docking stations, make sure they're properly connected and powered.
Display Settings:

Right-click on your desktop and select "Display settings."
Under "Multiple displays," choose how you want to display your screen:
Duplicate: Shows the same image on all screens.
Extend: Expands your desktop across all screens.
Second screen only: Uses only the external monitor(s).
Adjust the resolution and scaling for each monitor as needed.
Click "Identify" to see which monitor is which.
Graphics Drivers:

Make sure your laptop has the latest graphics drivers installed. You can usually find these on the website of your laptop manufacturer or the graphics card manufacturer (e.g., Intel, NVIDIA, AMD).
Windows Key Sequence:

Press the Windows logo key + Ctrl + Shift + B to reset your graphics driver. This can sometimes resolve display issues.
Hardware Troubleshooting:

If possible, try connecting the external monitors to another device to rule out any issues with the monitors themselves.
If you're using a docking station, try connecting the monitors directly to your laptop to see if the docking station is causing the problem.
Addressing the Display Timeout Issue

Power & Sleep Settings:

Go to "Settings" > "System" > "Power & sleep."
Adjust the "Screen" and "Sleep" settings to increase the time it takes for your display to turn off or for your laptop to go to sleep.
Screen Saver Settings:

Search for "Screen saver" in the Windows search bar and open "Change screen saver."
Adjust the "Wait" time to increase the delay before the screen saver activates. You can also choose to require a password on resume.
Additional Power Options:

Click on "Additional power settings" in the Power & sleep settings.
Select "Change plan settings" for your current power plan.
Adjust the "Turn off the display" and "Put the computer to sleep" settings as needed.
If the issue persists after trying these steps, please provide the following information:

Laptop model: This will help me find specific troubleshooting steps for your device.
External monitor models: Knowing the monitor models can help identify any compatibility issues.
Operating system: Please specify which version of Windows you're using (e.g., Windows 10, Windows 11).
With more information, I can provide more tailored assistance.
