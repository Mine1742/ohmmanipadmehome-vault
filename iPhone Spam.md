#iPhone

It's definitely frustrating to deal with that many spam text messages. Here's a breakdown of steps you can take to try and reduce or eliminate them:

1. Don't Engage:

Never reply: Even replying "STOP" can sometimes confirm to spammers that your number is active.1
Don't click links: These links can lead to phishing scams or malware downloads.2
Don't provide personal information: Legitimate companies rarely ask for sensitive information via text.3
2. Block the Numbers:

Most smartphones allow you to block individual numbers.4This is a basic but essential step.
The process varies slightly between iPhones and Android devices, but usually involves options within the messaging app.
https://www.donotcall.gov/register/reg.aspx
3. Report the Spam:

Forward to 7726 (SPAM): This is a crucial step. Forwarding the messages to this number helps your mobile carrier identify and block similar spam.5
Report through your messaging app: Many messaging apps have built-in options to report spam or junk.6
File a complaint with the FTC: You can report spam texts to the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.7
4. Use Spam Filtering Tools:

Carrier-provided tools:
Many mobile carriers (like Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile) offer free or paid spam filtering services.8 Check with your provider.
On your iPhone you can set it to only answer known numbers.  

Any caller whose number is not in your contacts list, Siri Suggestions, or recent outgoing calls is automatically directed to voicemail. You'll get a notification of the call afterward.
You can also funnel text messages from numbers that are not in your contacts to a separate list. Go to Settings > Messages and turn on Filter Unknown Senders.
To block someone in your Contacts lists, go to Settings > Phone > Blocked Contacts. Scroll to the bottom and tap Add New. That will bring up your Contacts list; select those you want to block. You can also get there via Settings > Messages > Blocked Contacts > Add New.

To block someone from a text, tap the number or name on the top of the screen, which will produce a drop-down menu with three options: audio, FaceTime, and Info. Tap Info. Tap Info again, scroll down, and tap Block this Caller.
5. Be Careful With Your Number:

Limit sharing: Be cautious about giving out your phone number, especially on online forms or to unfamiliar websites.
Review privacy policies: When providing your number, check the company's privacy policy to see how they use and share your information.
Key points to remember:

Spammers frequently change numbers, so blocking individual numbers may not solve the problem entirely.10
Using a combination of these methods is often the most effective approach.
It is very important to never give out personal information via text message, unless you are very certain of the recievers identity.
By taking these steps, you can significantly reduce the number of spam text messages you receive.
