[[Bluebeam]]
#bluebeam #onedrive 

To sync **Bluebeam** with **OneDrive** on an iPad, follow these steps to set up integration between Bluebeam and OneDrive for seamless access and editing of files:

---

### **1. Install and Log In to Bluebeam and OneDrive**
1. **Download Bluebeam Revu for iPad**:
   - Install the app from the **App Store**.

2. **Download Microsoft OneDrive**:
   - Install the **OneDrive** app from the **App Store**.

3. **Log In**:
   - Open the OneDrive app and log in with your Microsoft account.
   - Open Bluebeam Revu and log in with your Bluebeam ID, if required.

---

### **2. Link OneDrive to Bluebeam**
Bluebeam supports integration with cloud storage services like OneDrive:

1. **Open Bluebeam Revu**:
   - Launch the Bluebeam Revu app on your iPad.

2. **Access Cloud Storage Options**:
   - Tap the **File Access** icon (usually a folder icon).
   - Select **Cloud Storage** from the menu.

3. **Add OneDrive**:
   - Tap **Add Connection** or the **+** button.
   - Select **OneDrive** from the list of supported storage services.

4. **Authorize OneDrive**:
   - Log in to your OneDrive account when prompted.
   - Grant Bluebeam access to your OneDrive files.

5. **Verify Connection**:
   - Once connected, your OneDrive folders will appear in the Bluebeam app under **Cloud Storage**.

---

### **3. Open and Sync Files**
1. **Access Files**:
   - Navigate to the **OneDrive folder** from within Bluebeam.
   - Tap a file to open and edit it.

2. **Automatic Sync**:
   - Changes made to the file will automatically sync back to OneDrive, provided you have an active internet connection.

3. **Offline Files**:
   - To work offline, download files to your device:
     - Open the file and select **Save to Device** or **Make Available Offline**.

---

### **4. Organize and Save Files**
1. **Save Edited Files to OneDrive**:
   - After editing, tap **Save** or **Close** to sync changes back to OneDrive.

2. **Create Folders**:
   - You can organize files into folders directly in OneDrive or within Bluebeam’s OneDrive integration.

---

### **5. Troubleshooting Tips**
1. **Check OneDrive Account Permissions**:
   - Ensure your account has access to the files and folders you’re trying to sync.

2. **Update Apps**:
   - Make sure both Bluebeam Revu and OneDrive are updated to the latest versions.

3. **Reconnect OneDrive**:
   - If syncing issues occur, disconnect and reconnect your OneDrive account in Bluebeam.

4. **Check Internet Connection**:
   - Ensure your iPad is connected to a stable internet connection for syncing.

5. **Storage Space**:
   - Ensure there’s sufficient space in both OneDrive and your iPad for syncing files.
