# How to Safely Remove a SharePoint Shortcut from OneDrive

## 🎯 Goal

Remove a **SharePoint folder shortcut** from your **OneDrive** without deleting any actual files or folders stored in SharePoint.

---

## ✅ Recommended Method: Use OneDrive Web Interface

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open OneDrive in a browser**:
   - Go to: [https://onedrive.live.com](https://onedrive.live.com) (personal)
   - Or open OneDrive from your Microsoft 365 portal.

2. **Locate the SharePoint shortcut**:
   - These appear with a chain link 🔗 icon and may include the word “Shortcut”.

3. **Select the folder**:
   - Click once to highlight it.

4. **Remove the shortcut**:
   - Right-click the folder or click the toolbar's “...” menu.
   - Choose **“Remove shortcut”**.
   - ⚠️ Do **NOT** click “Delete” — this may delete content from SharePoint if you have full permissions.

---

## ❌ What Not To Do

- Do **not delete** the shortcut from File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac) unless you are sure it is a shortcut.
- Do **not use the OneDrive mobile app** for this — it may not clearly show what is a shortcut.

---

## 💡 Additional Notes

- Removing a shortcut affects **only your view** in OneDrive.
- The original SharePoint files remain safely stored in the shared document library.
- This action does **not affect** other users' access to the folder.
