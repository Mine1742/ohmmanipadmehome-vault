
# 🧰 Google Workspace: Initial Setup Guide for New Users

Welcome to Google Workspace! This guide walks you through the first-time setup steps to access and configure your account for productivity.

---

## ✅ 1. Sign In to Your Account

1. Open your browser and go to: [https://mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com)
2. Enter your **Google Workspace email address** (e.g., `yourname@yourcompany.com`)
3. Enter the **temporary password** provided by your admin
4. Follow the prompts to **set a new secure password**

---

## 2. Set Up 2-Step Verification (Optional but Recommended)

1. Visit: [https://myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security)
2. Under **"Signing in to Google"**, click **2-Step Verification**
3. Click **Get Started** and follow the steps to add:
   - A mobile phone for text codes
   - Google Authenticator or another TOTP app (optional)

---

## 📬 3. Access Gmail

1. Visit [https://mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com)
2. You’ll see your inbox
3. Configure:
   - Signature: ⚙️ Settings > See all settings > **General** > Signature
   - Vacation responder, density, and labels

---

## 📆 4. Access Calendar

1. Go to: [https://calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com)
2. Add important events or sync your work calendar
3. Set working hours and time zone under ⚙️ Settings

---

## 📁 5. Explore Google Drive

1. Visit: [https://drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)
2. Upload files or create Docs, Sheets, or Slides
3. Organize with folders and set permissions to share with your team

---

## 👥 6. Join Google Meet or Chat

- Meet: [https://meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com)
- Chat: [https://chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com)

Collaborate with teammates using built-in video and text communication.

---

## 📲 7. Set Up on Mobile (iOS/Android)

1. Install these apps from the App Store or Google Play:
   - Gmail
   - Google Calendar
   - Google Drive
   - Google Meet / Chat
2. Log in with your Google Workspace credentials
3. Allow necessary permissions

---

## 🔁 8. Sync Email with Outlook or Apple Mail (Optional)

Follow Google’s guide to [use Gmail with other mail clients](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7126229)

---

## 🛠 Tips for Productivity

- Use [Google Keep](https://keep.google.com) for notes
- Bookmark [https://workspace.google.com/dashboard](https://workspace.google.com/dashboard) to access all tools in one place
- Visit [https://support.google.com](https://support.google.com) for help articles and tutorials

---

## 🔐 Security Reminder

- Don’t share your password
- Log out of shared/public devices
- Use 2-step verification whenever possible
