# 🛠 Outlook Preview Pane Zoom/Scroll Issue Troubleshooting Guide

If you're unable to **zoom** or **scroll** through attachments in the Outlook preview pane, use the following steps to resolve the issue.

---

## 🧠 Symptoms

- Attachments (PDF, Word, Excel, etc.) can't be zoomed or scrolled in the reading/preview pane.
- Mouse scroll wheel or arrow keys do not work.
- Ctrl + Scroll does not zoom.

---

## ✅ Troubleshooting Steps

### 🔹 1. Open Attachment in a New Window
- Double-click the attachment or click **“Open in App”**
- This launches it in the native viewer (Word, Excel, Adobe Reader, etc.)
- If zoom/scroll works here, issue is isolated to Outlook preview pane

---

### 🔹 2. Ensure the Preview Pane Has Focus
- Click **inside the preview pane** to give it focus
- Use:
  - **Arrow keys** to scroll
  - **Page Up/Down**
  - **Ctrl + Mouse Wheel** to zoom

---

### 🔹 3. Check Outlook Add-ins
1. Go to **File > Options > Add-ins**
2. Click **Manage > COM Add-ins > Go**
3. Disable third-party or PDF-related add-ins (e.g., Nitro, Foxit, Adobe)
4. Restart Outlook

---

### 🔹 4. Repair Office Installation
1. Go to **Control Panel > Programs and Features**
2. Select **Microsoft 365 Apps**
3. Click **Change**
4. Choose:
   - **Quick Repair** (first)
   - Then **Online Repair** if issue persists

---

### 🔹 5. Switch to Classic (Legacy) Outlook
If you're using **New Outlook**, preview features may be limited.

1. Click **Help** (or top-right gear icon)
2. Select **"Revert to Legacy Outlook"**
3. Restart and test the preview again

---

### 🔹 6. Try in Outlook Web App (OWA)
Visit [https://outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com) and test previewing the attachment from your browser.

> If it works there, the issue is with the **desktop client only**.

---

## 🧠 Tip
Use **“Open in app”** or **“Open in browser”** for best experience when preview fails in Outlook.
