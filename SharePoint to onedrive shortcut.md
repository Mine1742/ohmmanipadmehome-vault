#sharepoint #onedrive 

## **Method 1: Add a Shortcut to OneDrive (Recommended)**

This method allows SharePoint files/folders to appear in your **OneDrive folder** without duplicating storage.

### **Steps:**

1. Open **SharePoint** in your web browser.
2. Navigate to the **document library** or **folder** you want to add.
3. Click on the **folder** or **document library**.
4. Click the **"Add shortcut to OneDrive"** button in the toolbar.
5. The shortcut will appear in **OneDrive (both online and in the synced folder on your PC/Mac).**

### **Where to Find It:**

- **Web:** Go to [OneDrive Online](https://onedrive.live.com/) → **Files** → You’ll see the SharePoint shortcut.
- **Windows:** Open **File Explorer > OneDrive – [Your Org Name]**.
- **Mac:** Open **Finder > OneDrive – [Your Org Name]**.

---

## **Method 2: Sync SharePoint with OneDrive (Local Access)**

If you want **offline access**, sync SharePoint libraries directly.

### **Steps:**

1. Open **SharePoint Online** and go to the **document library**.
2. Click **Sync** (located in the toolbar).
3. If prompted, allow **OneDrive to open** and sign in.
4. The **SharePoint library will sync** into your **OneDrive folder**.

### **Where to Find It:**

- Open **File Explorer** (Windows) or **Finder** (Mac).
- Look under **OneDrive – [Your Org Name]** for the synced SharePoint files.

---

## **Key Differences Between Shortcuts and Syncing**

|Feature|Add Shortcut to OneDrive|Sync with OneDrive|
|---|---|---|
|**Storage**|Uses OneDrive links (doesn't take space)|Downloads files locally|
|**Offline Access**|No (online-only)|Yes (files are stored locally)|
|**Best For**|Quick access without duplication|Editing files offline|

If you need **offline editing**, use **Sync**. If you want **quick access without using storage**, use **Shortcut**.
